# ObsidianRoll Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-18

ObsidianRoll ("the app") is a photo and file gallery that encrypts your media on your device and stores the encrypted data in your own Telegram account. It is published by gemxd ("we", "us"). Contact: open a discussion at https://github.com/pishabh625/obsidian-roll-docs/discussions.

**Short version:** we have no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. We cannot read your media, your file names, or your passphrase. The only data that reaches our server is a subscription purchase record, and only if you buy ObsidianRoll Plus.

## 1. How the app works

- You create a vault protected by a passphrase you choose. The passphrase and the encryption keys derived from it stay on your device. We never receive them.
- Photos, videos and files you add are split into chunks and encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with keys derived using Argon2id) before anything leaves it.
- The encrypted chunks are uploaded to your own Telegram account using Telegram's network protocol. Thumbnails, file names, albums and search data are kept in a database on your device.
- If you lose your passphrase and recovery phrase, your data cannot be recovered. We cannot reset or recover them.

## 2. Data we collect and how it is used

### Data that stays on your device
Your media metadata (file names, dates, albums, favourites, search index), the vault database, Telegram session data, app settings, and, if you turn on biometric unlock, a passphrase copy sealed by the Android Keystore. This data is not sent to us. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

### Data sent to Telegram
To sign in, the app sends your phone number and login code directly to Telegram, and uploads and downloads the encrypted chunks of your media. This is how the app stores your files. We do not receive or see this data. Telegram's own terms and privacy policy apply to it: https://telegram.org/privacy. You supply your own Telegram API credentials (from my.telegram.org); they are stored on your device.

### Data sent to our billing service (only if you buy Plus)
If you buy ObsidianRoll Plus, the app sends the following to our billing service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1, over HTTPS:

- the purchase token issued by Google Play;
- a random installation identifier generated by the app, and a one-time challenge value;
- your network address, which reaches the service as a normal part of any request and is used only to apply rate limits (stored as hashed counters; it is not logged).

We use this to verify your subscription with Google Play, acknowledge the purchase, and return a short-lived signed entitlement that unlocks Plus features. The purchase token is stored encrypted. It contains no name, email, media or vault information. Verified purchase records are deleted 60 days after the subscription expires. If you never buy Plus, the app does not send this data.

### Google Play
Purchases, subscription management and in-app review prompts are handled by Google Play and are governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

### What we do not do
We do not sell or share personal data. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or SMS.

## 3. Permissions

- **Internet:** to reach Telegram and, for Plus subscribers, our billing service.
- **Billing:** to purchase and restore Plus through Google Play.
- **Biometric:** optional fingerprint or face unlock.
- **Notifications and foreground service (data sync):** to show progress while backing up or downloading in the background.
- Files are added through Android's system picker. The app does not request broad storage access.

## 4. Sharing

We share nothing with third parties for their own purposes. The services described above (Telegram, Google Play, Cloudflare) process data only as needed to run the features you use. Your encrypted data is stored by Telegram in your own account; deleting it there removes it.

## 5. Security

Media is encrypted before upload. Network traffic uses encryption in transit (HTTPS for our service, Telegram's encrypted protocol for Telegram). Entitlements are signed and verified by the app. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for keeping your passphrase, recovery phrase and Telegram account safe.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- **On your device:** uninstalling the app, or using Settings, removes local data. Signing out of Telegram removes the session from the device.
- **In Telegram:** the encrypted chunks live in your Telegram account. Delete them in Telegram, or use the app's delete and trash features.
- **Billing records:** to ask us to delete the purchase record linked to your subscription, open a discussion at https://github.com/pishabh625/obsidian-roll-docs/discussions (do not post your purchase token or order details publicly; ask for a private channel in the discussion and we will arrange one) and include only that you want your billing record deleted. Otherwise records are deleted 60 days after expiry.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to processing. Because almost all of your data stays on your device or in your Telegram account, you can exercise most of these rights yourself. For the billing record, contact us through the discussions page above. We will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Children

ObsidianRoll is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect their data.

## 9. International transfers

Our billing service runs on Cloudflare's global network, so billing data may be processed in countries other than yours. Telegram and Google operate globally as well.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
