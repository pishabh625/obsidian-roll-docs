# Frequently asked questions

## Can you read my photos or files?
No. Everything is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, keys derived from your passphrase with Argon2id) before it is uploaded. We never receive your passphrase or keys.

## Where is my data stored?
Encrypted chunks are stored in your own Telegram account. Names, albums and the search index are kept in a database on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/).

## I forgot my passphrase. Can you reset it?
No. Zero-knowledge means nobody can recover it for you. Keep your recovery phrase somewhere safe and offline.

## Do I need a Telegram account?
Yes. ObsidianRoll signs in to your Telegram account to store and fetch the encrypted data. During setup you sign in with your phone number and the login code Telegram sends you. The app includes its own Telegram API credentials, so you do not need to create any. You can skip sign-in at first and connect Telegram later in Settings; backup starts once you are connected.

## What is free and what is Plus?
Free includes your originals, recovery and export, with limits of 1 GB per file and 4 GB of new imports per day, 2 upload and 2 download connections, and background backup on Wi-Fi only. Plus removes the file-size and daily import limits, adds backup over mobile data, up to 4 upload and 6 download connections, and keeps deleted items in trash for 365 days. New subscribers can try the annual plan free for 14 days through Google Play. Google Play asks for a payment method and charges the yearly price when the trial ends unless you cancel before then; the app reminds you 2 days before. Cancel any time in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions. If you cancel, Plus stays on until the trial or paid period ends, then Free limits apply to new imports. Your files always stay accessible. Prices are shown in the app by Google Play for your region.

## How do I cancel Plus?
In Google Play: Menu > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > ObsidianRoll > Cancel. Your media stays accessible if you cancel.

## How do I delete my data?
Delete items inside the app, delete the chunks in Telegram, or uninstall the app to remove local data. To delete the billing record linked to your subscription, see section 6 of the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/).

## Which Android versions are supported?
Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer.

## Something is broken. Where do I report it?
Open a [discussion](https://github.com/pishabh625/obsidian-roll-docs/discussions) or an [issue](https://github.com/pishabh625/obsidian-roll-docs/issues). Never post your passphrase, recovery phrase or purchase token.
